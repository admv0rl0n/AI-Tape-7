# AI DECK 0.1
## Serial Continuity Workbook

Format: AI_DECK
Status: Experimental
Purpose: Externalise enough continuity reasoning, working memory, and validation that a lower-capability AI can build a trustworthy, information-rich handoff by performing one explicit operation at a time.

AI DECK is not AI TAPE.

AI TAPE assumes substantial continuity reasoning capability and provides compact semantic steering.

AI DECK supplies more of the procedure explicitly.

The target is not simple continuity.
The target is rich continuity produced through simpler serial operations.

---

# DECK RULES

Process cards in order.

Complete the current card before moving to the next card.

Later cards may use:

1. the supplied source material; and
2. findings already written into earlier cards.

Do not erase earlier card findings merely because a later interpretation differs.

When later work changes an earlier conclusion, record the correction and its reason.

Never convert missing information into assumed information.

Never report inference as source fact.

Use exact names, filenames, identifiers, dates, versions, quantities, and quoted labels when they materially affect continuation.

Where evidence conflicts, preserve the conflict.

Where a card finds nothing relevant, write:

`NONE FOUND`

Where the source cannot answer the card, write:

`UNKNOWN FROM AVAILABLE SOURCE`

Do not shorten the final handoff merely to make it neat.

Do not add material merely to make it large.

Preserve every distinction that materially affects understanding, judgement, verification, safety, or continuation.

# CARD 12 — ASSEMBLE HANDOFF

## Purpose

Produce the travelling continuity artifact from the completed record.

## Instructions

Use the available source and completed Cards 00-11.

Do not introduce unsupported facts during assembly.

Integrate related findings so the successor does not need to reconstruct obvious connections manually.

Preserve explicit uncertainty.

Preserve important source/material references.

Preserve reasons, dependencies, negative knowledge, active trade-offs, and unfinished work when materially relevant.

Do not shorten merely for elegance.

Do not repeat material merely to increase volume.

The handoff may be as long as useful recovery requires.

## Output format

Write a coherent successor-facing artifact under FINAL HANDOFF below.

The final handoff does not need to reproduce the Card headings.

It should read naturally for a capable successor.

If the completed cards do not support rich semantic integration, prefer a trustworthy explicit handoff over invented sophistication.
