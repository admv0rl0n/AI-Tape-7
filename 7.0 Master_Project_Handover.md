00_HEADER

AI Tape is a long-running continuity research project exploring how useful project reality can survive discontinuity between AI participants, sessions, context windows, model families, and transfer mechanisms.

The project has passed through multiple major architectural eras.

The current conclusion is that continuity dimensions appear more stable than continuity techniques.

AI Tape 7.0 Brutal Core exists to provide a stable continuity floor while allowing continuity research to continue in Workshop space.

This artifact is intended to serve as a project baseline rather than a protocol specification.
