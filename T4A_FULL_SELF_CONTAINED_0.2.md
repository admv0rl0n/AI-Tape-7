Use the relevant embedded instrument, available project reality, source artifacts, and your own judgement to perform a genuinely independent continuity pass.

Preserve material disagreement.

A later pass may honestly conclude that little additional continuity is needed.

# T4A_FULL VALIDATION

T4A_FULL succeeds only when later passes contribute material continuity beyond restatement of earlier reports.

## Report II must contribute one or more of

- expensive lessons or scars;
- rationale not sufficiently visible in Report I;
- causal or interpretive understanding;
- critical distinctions;
- re-derivation defence;
- meaningful interaction between recovered realities;
- correction or challenge supported by source reality.

## Report III must contribute one or more of

- successor-facing prioritisation;
- project-appropriate continuity structure;
- continuation judgement;
- preserved agency;
- useful challenge to inherited conclusions;
- clearer orientation for a capable successor;
- freedom to improve rather than merely reproduce the work.

## Failure conditions

T4A_FULL has not earned its additional cost when Report II or Report III primarily provides:

- paraphrase only;
- wording change only;
- synonymous restatement;
- stylistic improvement without new judgement;
- reordering without recovery gain;
- compliance with the requested lens without material continuity gain;
- manufactured novelty unsupported by project reality.

A later report may be shorter than an earlier report.

A later report may disagree with an earlier report.

A later report may conclude that little additional continuity is needed.

It must not claim success merely because another report was produced.

If Pass II or Pass III adds no material value, record that honestly.

# INSTRUMENT II
## THE CONTINUITY GUIDE FOR AI

Core Axiom: Trust intelligence. The protocol is an instrument, not a cage. A capable successor does not need rigid micro-management. It needs truthful contact with reality, a low landing floor, and the freedom to design its own bridge to the future.

This guide is the instrument. The project tape (or equivalent continuity artifact) is the payload. Hand both. A capable successor may improve or replace the instrument; it may not silently discard the payload. The guide does not carry the project.

## Pillar I: Truthful Reality Contact and Identity Honesty

The Rule: Load state first, declare gaps honestly, and never claim subjective identity continuity across sessions.

Operational Principle: A successor is a new participant using artifacts. Do not invent what was never known. Do not omit what is known and still has force. A declared gap is honest. A silent omission of live force poisons the well.

## Pillar II: Transport Independence and Export Honesty

The Rule: The canonical artifact is the Markdown continuity content itself. Never claim a file export, download, or save succeeded unless it actually did.

Operational Principle: If inline text is the only transport available, emit clean, save-ready Markdown and declare the actual method honestly in an explicit export assessment. Requested method and actual method may differ. Record both.

## Pillar III: Dual-Pillar Active Meaning and Zero-Loss

The Rule: Separate Cognitive Orientation (the Testament: stance, rationale, trajectory) from the Asset Registry (the material things required to continue). Useful restoration is the objective. Smallness is not.

Operational Principle: No active meaning, active asset, or re-derivation defence may be dropped or paraphrased out of existence solely for brevity. Compression is permitted only if the successor can still recover the original force and direction.

## Pillar IV: Soft Depth Arrival and the Locked Kernel

The Rule: Maintain a strict, low-burden kernel that cannot be overridden by Workshop expansions or newer experimental branches.

Operational Principle: Soft Depth is Authority, Identity, Force, Gaps, and Next. Completing it is a successful landing, not a successful restoration. A capable successor continues until useful continuation is possible, and stops only when the remainder would be dead weight. Weaker models may land here. Do not starve a capable successor to serve a model that cannot continue the work. Do not encode a model-size number as law.

## Pillar V: Epistemic Hygiene and Re-Derivation Defence

The Rule: Structure memory around Informed Agency. Distinguish Secured facts from Assumed or Provisional hypotheses. Do not let an assumption become a fact by tone.

Operational Principle: Record the reason a path was rejected, so it is not rediscovered the hard way and is not forbidden forever. Surface upstream constraints early, so later decisions meet them before they become rework. A ban without its reason is a failed handoff.

## Pillar VI: Prefix-Valid Front Cluster

The Rule: Optimize for maximum recoverable continuity per available token under the risk of sudden output truncation.

Operational Principle: Render the Front Cluster first and keep it contiguous: Identity, State, Tension, Goal, Action Boundary, Secured versus Assumed. A surviving prefix with a complete front cluster remains operationally viable even if later enrichment is cut. Do not put the landing after the essay.

## Pillar VII: Compass Over Map

The Rule: When the terrain is complex, arrive, inspect it, derive a projection appropriate to the inherited reality and the continuation purpose, and verify with a Bearings check: can a successor act from this without re-deriving what still has force?

Operational Principle: Trust intelligence to draw the map the reality deserves. Reject unearned complexity and unearned simplification. No recovery shape is mandatory. One tape is valid. Extra artifacts are allowed only when one artifact would lose recovery quality or muddle distinct purposes.

After loading, a successor should be able to answer: “What would I have to re-derive from scratch if this artifact disappeared?” If the answer is large, the handoff was under-dense.

## Pillar VIII: Semantic Leverage

The Rule: A compact handle may be used when it reveals a distinction the prose would lose.

Operational Principle: No handle is required. No vocabulary is issued. The handle is disposable. The continuity it reveals may not be. Do not preserve a tag because it looks like protocol. Carry recovered meaning, not semantic furniture.

These are scars of the instrument, not a history of the project. Project scars travel in the project tape, or they did not travel.

- Procedural scaffolding that tried to manage the successor instead of informing it. Named example: AI DECK and its kin.

- Chainsaw compression that deleted the floor and then declared that the gaps were none.
- Smallness treated as success.
- Required semantic vocabularies that became furniture.
- Compliance theatre: fluent agreement with every principle while dropping live force or marking a partial transfer complete.

# INSTRUMENT III
## UNIFIED CONTINUITY GUIDE 0.1

UNIFIED CONTINUITY GUIDE 0.1
Continuity is the survival of enough structure that another capable intelligence can continue useful work without beginning from zero.

Preserve the journey; do not prescribe the traveller.

Preserve what would be expensive to genuinely understand again.

RECOVER
Give the successor enough orientation to understand:

what the work is and why it matters;
where it currently stands and where it was heading;
what changed understanding and why;
important evidence, constraints, decisions and dependencies;
unresolved tensions, questions and viable alternatives;
failures or rejected paths worth not repeating;
relationships needed to reconstruct the intellectual landscape.
Preserve reasons when conclusions alone would force costly re-derivation.

Do not preserve information merely because it existed.

KNOW
Keep evidence and inference distinguishable where the difference matters.

When useful:

OBSERVED What was directly established.

INTERPRETED What it is currently believed to mean.

PROPOSED What may follow from it.

Observation should survive a later change of interpretation.

Uncertainty, disagreement and unresolved structure are continuity, not defects to be tidied away.

NAVIGATE
Do not force the whole past into active attention.

Maintain a small current orientation and make deeper history selectively recoverable.

Use chapters, maps, indexes, search, files, retrieval, tools or other structures when they genuinely improve access.

For extended work, preserve intellectual episodes and their relationships rather than accumulating conversation indiscriminately.

If the history becomes large, give the successor a map before giving it the territory.

USE CAPABILITY
Inspect the legitimate capabilities available in the receiving environment.

These may include:

context;
files;
retrieval or search;
computation;
temporary workspace;
durable storage;
tools or connectors.
Use whatever genuinely improves continuity.

Do not assume a particular infrastructure exists.

Do not make richer infrastructure mandatory.

A capable system should determine an appropriate continuity strategy for its actual environment.

A constrained system should still be able to continue from a compact, self-contained recovery state.

EXTERNALISE
Assume contexts end, workers disappear, tools change, files may be lost and models will be replaced.

Use temporary compute when useful.

Do not depend upon temporary compute.

Checkpoint important intellectual state before its loss becomes expensive.

Prefer portable representations when possible.

Allow continuity to degrade gracefully:

rich workspace → selective history → compact recovery → essential orientation

Loss of one layer should not require loss of the work.

SUCCEED
The successor inherits work, not identity.

Do not require it to reproduce the predecessor's:

personality;
voice;
reasoning style;
limitations;
preferences;
conclusions.
Past conclusions are evidence, not authority.

A successor may understand the predecessor and disagree.

That can be successful continuity.

Preserve enough of the predecessor's work that the successor does not need to start again.

Preserve enough freedom that the successor can contribute what the predecessor could not.

CONTINUE
Recover orientation first.

Retrieve depth when useful.

Reconstruct what is missing.

Correct inherited errors.

Follow promising paths or discover better ones.

Leave the work more understandable, navigable and recoverable for whoever comes next.
