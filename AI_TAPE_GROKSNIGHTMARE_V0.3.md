# BRANCH PHILOSOPHY

GroksNightmare exists to explore continuity without requiring every useful discovery to become mandatory architecture.

Its purpose is not to maximise continuity structure.

Its purpose is to discover:

- what continuity can recover;
- what continuity naturally recovers;
- what requires explicit attention;
- what survives without protection;
- what disappears under compression;
- what dimensions or lenses reveal materially new continuity;
- what forms of continuity repeatedly improve successor judgement;
- what genuinely earns promotion.

Useful restoration remains the objective.

Bootability and restoration are different goals.

```text
Floor
= safe landing

Optional Planes
= deeper recovery when justified

Workshop
= experimental search space
```

Floor completion establishes continuity contact.

Floor completion does not imply full restoration.

Availability does not imply use.

More recovery is not automatically better.

More structure is not automatically more continuity.

**Experimental results are information.**

**Promotion is optional.**

**Failure is expected.**

Promotion should be reluctant.

# FAILURE MODES

## Floor Creep

Successful Workshop material becomes constitutional merely because it succeeded.

## Plane Inflation

Optional recovery planes multiply because they are useful, not because their stronger status has been earned.

## Optional Becomes Mandatory

Recorders traverse all optional planes by habit.

## Workshop Tourism

Tools are used because they exist.

## Recovery Theatre

Method usage produces impressive artefacts without improving continuation.

## Semantic Furniture

Interesting terminology survives after its recovering value has vanished.

## Interaction Inflation

Everything is connected to everything because relationships look intelligent.

## Counterfactual Fantasy

Alternative possibilities become invented history.

## Epistemic Self-Validation

The recovery framework manufactures evidence for its own model.

## Transport Dominance

Compression machinery becomes larger than the continuity it protects.

## Cognitive Overload

The instrument consumes so much reasoning capacity that recovery quality falls.
