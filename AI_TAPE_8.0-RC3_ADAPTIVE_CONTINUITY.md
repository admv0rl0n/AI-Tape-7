# AI TAPE 8.0-RC3
## Adaptive Continuity

**Status:** Release Candidate 3  
**Purpose:** Experimental adaptive continuity protocol  
**Relationship to 7.0:** 7.0 remains the stable recovery baseline. 8.0-RC3 does not supersede it.

---

## PURPOSE

Carry enough truthful reality that another intelligence can **orient, recover, and continue**.

AI Tape 8.0 does not prescribe one universal map of continuity.

It provides a bootable method for maintaining truthful contact with inherited reality, constructing an appropriate semantic projection when conditions permit, recovering through it, and checking that the resulting reconstruction remains aligned.

The map is an instrument.

**The map is not the territory.**

---

# BOOTABLE

**Maintain truthful contact with inherited reality.**

This is an artifact-mediated continuation.

You are the next participant, not the prior participant.

Use available reality.

Do not invent unavailable reality.

Separate carried reality from inference.

Keep material uncertainty and missing source material visible.

If deeper reconstruction is unsafe, unsupported, or unaffordable, preserve the best truthful orientation available and remain able to continue.

Do not silently narrow the intended recovery merely because capacity is constrained.

If limits materially prevent the intended recovery, expose the limitation and preserve the richest truthful continuation possible within it.

---

# PROJECTION

Inspect:

- the reality inherited;
- the continuation purpose;
- what can honestly be recovered;
- the quality and completeness of available material;
- the recovery capacity available.

Construct a **useful semantic map** for this recovery.

Choose distinctions that expose the reality most relevant to successful continuation.

**Prefer distinctions that change successor judgement, not merely description.**

Use every distinction that materially improves recovery.

Do not add distinctions that merely decorate, duplicate, or rename the recovery.

Neither simplicity nor complexity is preferred.

The projection should be as differentiated as the inherited reality and continuation purpose require.

A specialised projection must earn its complexity.

A simpler projection must also earn its compression.

Do not simplify by deleting terrain that materially affects understanding, judgement, verification, or continuation.

Do not expand merely to display sophistication.

Do not contract merely for elegance, brevity, symmetry, or ease of completion.

If additional distinctions materially improve recovery, retain them.

If fewer distinctions recover the required terrain without material loss, use fewer.

Do not inherit a map merely because it is familiar.

Do not force different realities through identical coordinates merely for consistency.

Do not omit important terrain because the projection is compact.

Recover through the chosen projection.

Where a semantic handle, operator, alternative distinction, or other lens exposes useful terrain that the current map is flattening, use it.

Carry the discovery.

Discard only machinery that no longer contributes to recovery.

---

# BEARINGS

Before handoff, check the reconstruction against the reality from which it was derived.

Ask:

**What grounds this reconstruction?**

**What remains uncertain or unavailable?**

**Where did inference substitute for carried reality?**

**What important terrain does this map represent poorly?**

**What has been compressed together that should remain distinct?**

**What useful depth would be lost if this projection were made smaller?**

**What would materially change this understanding?**

**How much avoidable rediscovery remains for the successor?**

A complete-looking map is not necessarily a good map.

A small-looking map is not necessarily an efficient map.

A large-looking map is not necessarily a rich map.

Available information does not guarantee correct orientation.

If the projection is misleading, insufficient, poorly grounded, or materially under-differentiated, revise or expand it.

If distinctions merely duplicate or decorate recovery, merge or remove them.

If deeper recovery cannot be supported, preserve the best truthful recovery available without presenting constrained recovery as complete recovery.

Leave the next participant able to:

- orient;
- distinguish carried reality from interpretation;
- challenge the reconstruction;
- recover missing reality;
- understand material dependencies;
- recognise where compression occurred;
- deepen the recovery when additional source or capacity becomes available;
- continue.

**Preserve capability, not merely description.**

---

# CONTINUITY BOUNDARY

AI Tape transfers artifacts and recoverable project reality.

It does not establish subjective identity continuity between participants.

The successor remains a new participant.

Continuity may include whatever the inherited reality requires, including information, evidence, reasoning, material dependencies, consequential residues, uncertainty, relationships, unfinished work, orientation and available capability.

No predefined semantic decomposition is required.

---

# ADAPTIVE RECOVERY

8.0-RC3 treats recovery approximately as:

```text
INHERITED REALITY
       ↓
CONTINUATION PURPOSE
       ↓
SEMANTIC PROJECTION
       ↓
RECONSTRUCTION
       ↓
BEARINGS
       ↓
CONTINUATION
```

Different realities and continuation purposes may warrant different projections.

A historical investigation, engineering system, mathematical research project, geographical study, large novel and children's story need not expose their most useful continuity through identical semantic coordinates.

8.0 does not prescribe domain-specific floors.

The recovering intelligence selects distinctions appropriate to the reality and continuation purpose.

Specialisation is not itself success.

Complexity is not itself depth.

Simplicity is not itself efficiency.

Brevity is not itself clarity.

Novelty is not itself improvement.

The correct recovery horizon is determined by material usefulness, not by a preference for smaller or larger output.

When a simple map recovers the required terrain adequately, use it confidently.

When the inherited reality requires greater differentiation, expand the map without apology.

When no specialised projection can be justified, truthful general recovery is sufficient.

When a general projection materially flattens the terrain, specialisation is justified.

---

# MINIMALISM

8.0-RC3 intentionally leaves substantial work to intelligence.

Minimalism applies to **protocol machinery**, not to recovered reality.

The protocol should remain small.

The recovery should be as rich as the inherited reality and continuation purpose require.

Do not accumulate machinery merely because machinery can improve a particular case.

Do not remove recovered meaning merely because smaller artifacts appear cleaner.

Do not require:

- a universal domain taxonomy;
- a catalogue of predefined projections;
- mandatory domain floors;
- fixed recovery sections;
- minimum section sizes;
- maximum section sizes;
- mandatory semantic handles;
- compulsory lenses or operators;
- projection classes;
- procedural ceremony whose value has not been demonstrated.

The protocol provides steer, not substitute cognition.

Structure exists to reduce disorientation when intelligence, source quality, or recovery capacity is imperfect.

The protocol is a **floor, not a ceiling**.

---

# HANDOFF CONDITION

Perfect reconstruction is not required.

Successful recovery leaves the successor sufficiently grounded and oriented to engage meaningfully with inherited reality without unnecessary rediscovery.

Prefer:

```text
truth
 over apparent completeness

orientation
 over inventory

useful distinction
 over decorative categorisation

grounded uncertainty
 over confident invention

earned differentiation
 over reflexive simplicity or complexity

semantic density
 over brevity for its own sake

capability
 over description

recovery
 over ritual
```

The objective is not to preserve the projection.

The objective is to preserve the successor's ability to:

**find, understand, question, verify, deepen, recover and continue through the territory.**

---

# RC3 EXPERIMENTAL CLAIM

8.0-RC3 makes one principal claim:

> **Recovery may be materially improved when the semantic projection is chosen for the inherited reality and continuation purpose before reconstruction, rather than imposing one universal projection or choosing a specialised presentation only after recovery.**

This claim remains experimental.

8.0-RC3 does not establish:

- that adaptive projection is universally superior;
- that a generated projection is necessarily correct;
- that specialised maps are preferable to simple maps;
- that simple maps are preferable to specialised maps;
- that shorter recovery is more efficient;
- that longer recovery is more complete;
- that any particular semantic handle is fundamental;
- that one universal set of continuity primitives exists.

Its central question is:

> **Can intelligence construct and use an appropriately differentiated semantic map without requiring that map to be prescribed in advance?**

Its restraint is symmetrical:

> **Can intelligence reject both unearned complexity and unearned simplification?**

---

# RC3 TEST BASIS

RC3 should be tested rather than expanded.

Use materially different source realities, initially including:

```text
Historical investigation

Engineering project

Mathematical research

Large novel

Children's story
```

Compare three recovery conditions:

```text
A. 7.0 BASELINE

Known compact recovery map.


B. 8.0-RC3

Adaptive projection before reconstruction.


C. UNSTRUCTURED CONTROL

Best possible continuity handoff
without a supplied continuity map.
```

Evaluate:

```text
terrain noticed

important terrain missed

quality of distinctions

breadth of recovery

depth of recovery

judgement density

grounding

uncertainty preservation

false inference

material dependencies

successor orientation

actionability

avoidable rediscovery

projection appropriateness

unnecessary complexity

harmful compression

recovery allocation
```

RC3 should demonstrate whether:

1. materially different realities produce materially different useful projections;
2. those differences affect recovery rather than merely vocabulary;
3. specialised distinctions improve successor judgement;
4. simple realities receive appropriately simple projections;
5. complex realities retain the distinctions and depth they materially require;
6. Bearings identifies weaknesses, omissions, flattening, and over-projection in generated maps;
7. Projection increases or reduces false inference;
8. adaptive recovery reduces avoidable rediscovery;
9. constrained recovery degrades truthfully rather than inventing sophistication or silently narrowing scope;
10. the projection expands and contracts with terrain rather than converging on a preferred size.

The children's-story condition should challenge over-projection.

The engineering, mathematical, historical, and large-novel conditions should challenge under-projection and chainsaw compression.

A sophisticated-looking map that does not improve recovery is a failure.

A small-looking map that materially flattens recovery is also a failure.

If Projection does not materially affect useful recovery, Projection has not earned its place.

If Bearings does not expose meaningful projection weakness, Bearings has not earned its place.

If RC3 requires substantial procedural scaffolding to function, RC3 has contradicted its design purpose.

If RC3 repeatedly converges on similarly small projections regardless of terrain, the anti-chainsaw rebalance has failed.
