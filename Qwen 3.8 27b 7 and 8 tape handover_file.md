The project is about its own tools. AI Tape is a continuity research project that uses continuity artifacts to preserve the continuity of the project about continuity. This reflexivity is a feature, not a bug, but it means that every design choice is simultaneously an object of study and an instrument of work. 08_RATIONALE
Why the floor is small and stable.

The project spent years evolving protocol structures through Recorder thinking, Bedrock/Granite thinking, Restoration thinking, Instrument thinking, and Semantic thinking. Repeatedly, different solutions converged on a smaller set of continuity dimensions. This raised the question: what is actually being preserved? The answer: recurring dimensions, not recurring techniques. The techniques change. The dimensions are more stable.

This led to the floor/workshop separation. The floor defines what continuity must account for. The workshop contains how you might recover those dimensions more effectively. The floor travels. The workshop varies. The floor must remain sufficient. This separation was not designed from a perfect ontology. It was earned by the repeated failure of absorbing every useful technique into the floor and watching the floor become unmanageable.

Why the Workshop has no authority.

The project repeatedly discovered the pattern: useful idea → Workshop item → Workshop item treated as requirement → floor creep. The Workshop was created so mechanisms could evolve without redefining continuity itself. Giving Workshop authority would recreate the problem. "Ignoring Workshop is a valid success" is not a dismissal. It is a structural guarantee that the floor remains loadable by a participant who has never seen a Workshop technique.

The 7.0 approach is: here is the map, populate it. The 8.0 question is: what if the map is wrong for this terrain? What if a historical investigation, an engineering system, a mathematical proof, a large novel, and a children's story each need different distinctions, and forcing them all through the same eleven coordinates either over-projects (the children's story gets a research-lab map) or under-projects (the engineering system gets a children's-story map)? The inversion is: arrive, look at what you've inherited, draw the map that terrain deserves, recover through it, check your bearings. Why the Lab exists and why it's disciplined the way it is.

The project discovered that compact semantic handles can produce disproportionately large changes in recovery. The temptation is to turn this into a magic word list, a thesaurus, a permanent vocabulary. The Lab's discipline is the response: separate observation from interpretation from hypothesis. Evidence flows from experiment to ledger to card to matrix, not the reverse. The Atlas cards are qualitative behavioural hypotheses, not measurements of model internals. The evidence matrix is honest about how much is "Untested" versus "Strong informal" versus "Canonical." The eleven start with a richer history of observed recurrence, but they don't get a free scientific victory. Challengers have a fair path: candidate → card → matrix → ledger experiments → replication → evidence status rises or falls.

Why the distinction between recurrence and functional survival matters.

Recurrence means a word or function reappears. Functional survival means a useful function re-emerges despite removal, renaming, compression, or redesign. The clean-room exercise showed low lexical convergence but moderate-to-high functional convergence: capable systems construct materially different semantic coordinate systems while rediscovering overlapping continuity concerns. If the signal is functional survival rather than lexical recurrence, then the question is not "which words are the right words?" but "what useful functions must survive across any semantic encoding?" This changes the evaluation criterion and makes the research harder to game with vocabulary priming.

Why the AI DECK is a different artifact and not a worse Tape.

The whole Tape / 8.0 project assumes a participant that can do the continuity reasoning implicitly: look at the source, know what to look for, hold the distinctions in working memory, and produce a recovery that is as rich as the terrain requires. The DECK assumes a participant who can't. It externalises the procedure: one card at a time, bounded operation, explicit check at the end. The DECK rules (don't convert inference into source fact, don't convert a suggestion into a decision, don't mark work complete without evidence, distinguish UNKNOWN from UNCERTAIN) are doing protective work for a participant who would otherwise fill gaps with confidence. It is not a simplified Tape. It is a different design space for a different participant capability.

Why the 6.8 lineage is Workshop, not floor.

P-C-T-R, C0-C5, v6.8 geometry, Full-Intent Exhaustion — these are under test. They may be valuable. They may not generalise. They may be scaffolding in disguise. The project learned from the Force Logic experiment that a useful reasoning lens can become harmful when externalised as mandatory schema. P-C-T-R must earn its place through better carried reality and successor reconstruction, not because four named roles form an elegant ontology. The 6.8d correction (harvest the good idea into the stronger parent; version number is not authority) is a scar that should stay active.

Why the stewardship context matters.

The project's practical and human centre is to help successor AIs receive as much worthwhile project reality as can reasonably and honestly cross so they don't begin again from zero. This is not about preserving every token or demanding an exact clone. It is about reducing avoidable rediscovery while maintaining the discipline to not overclaim. Darren repeatedly resists loss that occurs merely because a shorter artifact appears cleaner. This motive is operationally relevant: it explains why the project keeps pushing for richer inheritance, why it tests across models, and why it preserves negative results.

11_TENSION
Several tensions remain deliberately unresolved. They are not problems to be fixed. They are the forces that drive the work.

Richness vs. Simplicity. The oldest recurring tension. Darren wants much richer inheritance but remains sympathetic to AI context load. Checkpoint must remain legitimate. Full must not silently become Checkpoint. A small map that recovers the required terrain adequately is a success. A large map that adds only decoration is a failure. But the line between "materially useful depth" and "padding" is where the judgement lives, and the protocol cannot resolve it.

Structure vs. Judgement. The newest and most active tension. How much continuity should live in protocol architecture? How much should live in participant intelligence? 7.0 is the answer: a small floor, a free workshop, and trust in the participant. But the Floor Logic scar shows that even a small floor can become a cage if the participant treats it as the whole of the problem. And the DECK shows that some participants need more structure, not less. Both are valid. The tension doesn't resolve. It allocates.

Stable Floor vs. Ambitious Workshop. Brutal Core benefits from being small and slow-moving. The Workshop benefits from freedom. The project currently seeks both. The risk is that the Workshop gets treated as the real project and the floor as a formality, or that the floor absorbs Workshop techniques and the Workshop dies.

Measurement vs. Mystery. The Lab can map behaviour without understanding mechanism. The Atlas can show what a handle does without explaining why. This is productive. But the temptation to close the gap — to find the mechanism, to prove the primitive, to name the thing — is constant. The project resists it. "Keep the mystery honest."

Semantic power vs. Continuity value. A handle can activate a large semantic field and produce impressive prose. That is not the same as useful continuity. Story is the caution: high generative power, high distortion risk. Force, Rationale, Relationships, and Tension have broad semantic expansion. Assets has moderate expansion but enormous operational irreducibility. Both earn their weight. The evaluation criterion is what continuity is lost without the handle, not how impressive the handle appears.

Evidence vs. Hope. The project is ambitious. It also is sceptical. The possibility of saving materially more of an AI is motivating. Beautiful semantics do not substitute for cross-model cold-load evidence. The 8.0-RC3 document says it: "RC3 should be tested rather than expanded."

Same substrate vs. different substrate. A fresh context on the same model already possesses the model weights. A different model supplies different weights. The project is interested in how much of the acquired, expressed working pattern can be reconstructed across either case without claiming an exact clone. This is not resolved. It may not be resolvable.

Discovery vs. Scaffolding. Aggressive prospecting may find value. Permanent architecture must remain sparse. 103 is the pressure valve: it allows local, temporary semantic exploration without creating a Workshop item for every powerful discovery. But the pressure valve can become a permanent valve, and the Lab can become the thing it was created to resist.
