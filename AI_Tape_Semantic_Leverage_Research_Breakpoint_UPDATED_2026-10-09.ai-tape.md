## 05_FORCE

## 08_RATIONALE

The current eleven were not primarily invented from a perfect ontology. Their semantic functions repeatedly appeared during real continuity work and survived enough redesign that recurrence itself became noteworthy.

That observation motivated the Primitive Flashlight: a qualitative research probe examining activation radius, functional irreducibility, recombination, cross-context reach, semantic cost, predicted ablation loss, functional survival and distortion risk.

Calibration against the base eleven suggested primitives can earn value in different ways. Force, Rationale, Relationships and Tension have broad semantic expansion. Authority, Identity, State, Gaps and Assets strongly ground continuity. Questions and Next preserve unfinished reality and agency. Therefore high semantic "explosion" is not the only useful signature.

Mixed-word probes rejected weak controls such as Gravel while repeatedly finding potentially valuable candidates such as Direction and Evidence. Subsequent prospecting surfaced handles including Survival, Commitment, Provenance and Reversibility. None is promoted by these observations.

A key distinction emerged:

Recurrence means a word or function reappears.
Functional survival means a useful function re-emerges despite removal, renaming, compression or redesign.

This may be a stronger research signal than lexical frequency alone.

## 103_SEMANTIC_PROSPECTING — CANDIDATE

Purpose: Some compact semantic handles may reveal useful continuity that remains latent after ordinary recovery.

A handle may be a noun, verb, relationship or short phrase. Use judgement. Do not search for quantity.

When deeper recovery remains useful:

1. Notice a few promising semantic handles suggested by the material or by weaknesses in current recovery.
2. Ask: What does this make visible that the current recovery has not?
3. Apply handles likely to reveal materially useful distinctions, relationships or meaning.
4. Promote only recovered continuity that strengthens the floor.
5. Continue while additional handles provide meaningful new recovery.
6. Stop when further handles mainly produce duplication, filler, weak distinction, decorative interpretation, unnecessary narrative or negligible gain.

Guidance:
- Do not trawl vocabulary.
- No handle is required.
- Different participants may discover different useful handles.
- Finding no worthwhile additional handle is valid success.
- A useful handle may illuminate one project and add nothing to another.
- Do not preserve a handle merely because it produced an interesting thought.

Principle:

The handle is disposable.
The continuity it reveals may not be.
Use the few semantic handles that earn their weight.
Carry recovered continuity, not semantic furniture.

Current interpretation: 103 may operate as a small room opening many possible temporary rooms without permanently building them into Tape. It may also act as an anti-scaffolding pressure valve by reducing pressure to create a Workshop item for every powerful semantic discovery. This remains a hypothesis.

## RESEARCH UPDATE — CLEAN-ROOM, TERRAIN, OPERATORS, AND 8.0 PROJECTION

Status: exploratory update added after the 2026-10-07 breakpoint. This section extends the breakpoint; it does not rewrite the evidence that preceded it.

### Observation / Interpretation / Hypothesis discipline

The existing evidence-first rule remains binding for this research artifact: experiments feed the Evidence Ledger; interpretation follows. The source breakpoint explicitly separates observation, interpretation and hypothesis, treats the incumbent eleven as reference controls rather than scientifically proven eternal primitives, and warns that same-recorder comparisons are not independent evidence.

### 1. Words, functions, terrain, maps

A useful separation emerged during substitution, ablation, semantic-handle, and clean-room work:

```text
REALITY
  -> recurring TERRAIN
  -> useful FUNCTIONS
  -> alternative MAPS / projections
  -> semantic HANDLES
  -> optional INSTRUMENTS / OPERATORS
```

Working meanings:

- Reality: the actual inherited project and its material/source conditions.
- Terrain: recurring regions of concern that appear relevant to successful continuation.
- Functions: cognitive/recovery work performed over that terrain.
- Maps: semantic coordinate systems used to expose the terrain.
- Handles: compact words or phrases that make some terrain/functions salient.
- Instruments/operators: handles used to inspect, relate, transform, or navigate recovered reality.

This is Lab vocabulary only. It is not claimed as model-internal architecture or travelling Tape law.

### 2. Full-eleven ablation pilot

An initial ablation pack removed each canonical 7.0 label in turn. A subsequent review observed substantial apparent functional migration. Authority, Identity, Rationale and Tension appeared especially recoverable; State, Gaps, Assets, Relationships and Questions appeared partly recoverable; Next appeared weakest in that run.

However, the pilot was methodologically contaminated: several ablation conditions retained or deliberately relocated the omitted function in neighbouring language. It therefore tested lexical removal more cleanly than functional removal.

Evidence update:

```yaml
lexical_irreducibility_of_eleven: weakened
functional_overlap: supported_exploratorily
functional_irreducibility: not_established
Next_distinctiveness: preliminary_signal_only
```

### 3. Next / Agency / Direction

The continuation handle was explored through a three-way comparison:

```text
Next      -> immediate transition / movement
Agency    -> capability / available choice
Direction -> trajectory / bearing
```

Same-recorder observation suggested these are not simple synonyms. Next produced the strongest immediate-action pull; Agency opened the broadest capability/option space; Direction preserved trajectory while reducing immediate-action pressure.

Interpretation: Next may be grammatically unusual among the incumbent handles because it performs a more dynamic transition function. This remains a hypothesis.

### 4. Noun-like semantic field probes

Five deliberately unusual handles were explored over the same broad project reality:

- Palimpsest -> layers, overwritten traces, buried surviving influence.
- Hysteresis -> path-dependent present behaviour.
- Mycelium -> distributed and indirect continuity.
- Liminality -> threshold and in-between conditions.
- Anamnesis -> latent/reconstructive recovery from traces.

The main observation was not that one word won. The handles appeared to produce recognisably different recovery signatures.

A separate Copilot independently selected `Palimpsest` when asked for a strange powerful word for a similar continuity problem and described a closely related signature involving layers, overwriting, traces and old structures influencing new ones. This is notable recurrence but not proof of privileged lexical status. Shared model lineage, task wording, cultural language and project context remain confounds.

### 5. Operator probes

A separate family of relational/dynamic handles was tested:

```text
SURVIVES   -> what persists through change?
CAUSES     -> what produced what?
ENABLES    -> what becomes possible?
DEPENDS ON -> what requires what?
DESPITE    -> what remains true under opposition?
```

Same-recorder results suggested a useful behavioural distinction:

```yaml
semantic_regions:
  typical_form: noun_like_handles
  tentative_effect: change_what_becomes_salient

semantic_dynamics:
  typical_form: relational_or_operator_handles
  tentative_effect: change_which_relations_or_transformations_become_salient
```

`SURVIVES` produced the strongest direct link to functional survival. `DESPITE` produced a distinctive qualified-truth-under-opposition signature. `ENABLES` highlighted affordances/capability. `DEPENDS ON` strongly exposed prerequisites. `CAUSES` produced strong causal chains but also the clearest retrospective-causality risk.

`SURVIVES + DESPITE` together produced a distinct "resilient survival" signature: what materially persists across a change despite identifiable counterpressure. The pair also produced high narrative-bias risk and therefore requires hostile testing where things genuinely die and conflicts genuinely resolve.

### 6. Clean-room continuity designs

Two Copilot clean-room exercises barred the incumbent 7.0 vocabulary and asked for continuity foundations from first principles. Lexical overlap was low; functional overlap was substantial.

One design produced a PRISM-style floor:

```text
ORIGIN
NOW
WEIGHT
TRACE
BONDS
ABSENCE
MATTER
FRONTIER
AFFORDANCE
```

Another independently produced a navigation-oriented design including:

```text
Terrain / World
Signal / Ground
Fractures / Faultlines
Momentum
Constraints
Echoes / Anchors
Bearings
Drift
Preservation
Recovery
```

The strongest clean-room observation is:

```yaml
lexical_convergence: low
functional_convergence: moderate_to_high
orientation_convergence: surprisingly_high
```

Interpretation: capable systems can construct materially different semantic coordinate systems while still rediscovering overlapping continuity concerns. The continuity problem itself may constrain the solution space. This is an exploratory interpretation, not demonstrated ontology.

### 7. Notable differentiations from clean-room comparison

Several distinctions appeared useful because they separate terrain often flattened together:

```text
Signal  != Weight
How supported? vs How consequential?

Absence != Drift
Reality unavailable vs interpretation moving off-course despite available reality.

Now != Direction != Momentum != Affordance != Next
Location vs trajectory vs movement already underway vs available capability vs immediate transition.

Trace != Echo != Palimpsest != Hysteresis != Survives
Consequential residue vs recurrence vs buried layer vs path effect vs persistence through change.
```

`Bearings` emerged as a notable second-order function: not merely where the successor is, but how it can tell whether its reconstruction remains aligned with inherited reality.

### 8. Emerging navigation interpretation

Both clean-room designs independently leaned toward orientation/navigation rather than pure inventory. This produced a new exploratory distinction:

```text
Ledger continuity:
  preserve facts, context and records; success emphasises retention.

Navigational continuity:
  preserve orientation, judgement, reality contact and capability; success emphasises useful recovery.
```

These are not proposed protocol classes. They describe different centres of gravity observed in recovery design.

### 9. 8.0 research inversion

A new major-version research direction emerged:

```text
7.0: carry a stable known map.
8.0: arrive safely, derive a map appropriate to inherited reality and continuation purpose, recover through it, then check bearings.
```

The central experimental hypothesis is:

> Recovery may be materially improved when semantic projection is chosen before reconstruction, rather than forcing all realities through one universal projection or selecting a specialised presentation only after recovery.

7.0 remains the known stable recovery baseline and is not invalidated by this work.

### 10. 8.0 RC1 / RC2 design state

The emerging 8.0 candidate intentionally remains small. Its operational spine is:

```text
BOOTABLE -> PROJECTION -> BEARINGS
```

Key design invariants:

- Maintain truthful contact with inherited reality.
- A successor is a new participant using artifacts.
- Do not invent unavailable reality.
- Separate carried reality from inference.
- Construct a compact semantic map appropriate to both inherited reality and continuation purpose when recovery conditions permit.
- Prefer distinctions that change successor judgement, not merely description.
- A specialised projection must earn its complexity; if it does not clearly improve recovery, prefer the simpler projection.
- The map is an instrument, not the territory.
- Bearings asks what grounds the reconstruction, what remains unavailable/uncertain, where inference substituted for carried reality, what important terrain the map represents poorly, what would materially change understanding, and how much avoidable rediscovery remains.
- Preserve capability, not merely description.
- Do not ship domain taxonomies, projection catalogues, mandatory semantic handles, or Workshop machinery without evidence.

### 11. First 7.0 vs 8.0 RC1 comparison

Using AI Tape itself as source terrain, one Copilot comparison found that 7.0 and 8.0 naturally selected different recovery centres.

7.0 produced a compact project-oriented recovery around project standing, current situation, active importance, missingness, materials, reasoning, connections, open work, competing pressures and continuation.

8.0 RC1 derived the projection:

```text
Terrain
Trace
Projection
Bearings
Frontier
```

and foregrounded historical residue, map selection, orientation, drift risk and research frontier.

Preliminary interpretation:

```yaml
7_0_observed_tendency: stable_continuity_record
8_0_RC1_observed_tendency: navigational_interpretive_recovery
projection_materially_changed_attention: yes
projection_materially_changed_structure: yes
demonstrated_superiority: no
```

Principal confound: AI Tape itself already contains extensive projection/terrain/semantic research, so a genuinely unrelated-domain test is required before claiming generalisation.

### 14. Updated experiment backlog

Priority experiments:

1. Compare 7.0, 8.0 adaptive projection, and an unstructured handoff control on identical sources.
2. Test materially different terrains: historical investigation, engineering, mathematical research, large novel, children's story.
3. Use the children's-story condition as an anti-overengineering test: successful Projection should permit genuinely simple maps.
4. Test weak recovery conditions: partial source, uncertain provenance, constrained recovery capacity, and degraded history.
5. Test Bearings against confidently wrong/elegant projections, not merely incomplete ones.
6. Repeat clean-room map construction across different AI lineages; compare functions/territories after generation rather than priming vocabulary beforehand.
7. Run hostile `SURVIVES + DESPITE` tests where important functions truly disappear and tensions genuinely resolve.
8. Separate Signal from Weight experimentally using cases with high-signal/low-consequence and low-signal/high-consequence information.
9. Test Bearings vs Drift as calibration/recovery functions distinct from simple missingness.
10. Compare Trace, Echo, Palimpsest, Hysteresis and Survives on the same historical source to measure whether their beam shapes remain distinct.

### 15. Current breakpoint

The Lab's strongest current hypothesis is no longer "find the correct semantic words."

It is:

> Useful continuity may have recurring functional terrain. Different semantic maps can cover that terrain differently, and compact handles or operators may expose features that a given map otherwise flattens.

8.0 turns one part of that hypothesis into a falsifiable protocol experiment: can intelligence choose an appropriate map before reconstruction, remain grounded through Bearings, and improve useful recovery without rebuilding scaffolding?

Current posture:

```text
Preserve 7.0 as the stable baseline.
Keep Atlas/Evidence methodology evidence-first.
Treat clean-room convergence as exploratory.
Test projection cross-domain.
Prefer simpler maps when specialisation does not help.
Promote rarely.
Keep the mystery honest.
```
